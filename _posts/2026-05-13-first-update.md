The idea came from my weird obsession of jellyfish. One of my favorite activity is to sit in an aquarium for a whole day, doing nothing just to watch jellyfish swimming and floating. Inspired by Boids algothrium that Christopher mentioned on lecture, I decided to creat my own swarm of jellyfish.
The initial plan is to create an interactive jellyfish simulation in Processing. The main feature is a swarm of jellyfish floating in an underwater scene, using Boids flocking algorithm to make them move collectively, and each jellyfish’s bell and tentacles would naturally float along with water. Adjust water current to influence the moving direction of the entire group of jellyfish, and move the mouse to attract or repel jellyfishes. Let's see if I successfully acheive all of features above:)
