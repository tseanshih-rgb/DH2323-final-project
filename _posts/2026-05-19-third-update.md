---
I decide to use another approach, TRIANGLE_STRIP, to draw the bell. In this way, the surface is smoother and more seamless, and the problem of latency is solved. 
Also, I add more layers and halos to the bell, creating a glowing-in-the-dark effect.
Ambient light, point light and specular light are added.

After reconsidering the project goal and feedbacks from Christopher, I decide to focus on rendering techniques instead of boids algorithm.
